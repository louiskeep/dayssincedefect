How to play

📅 Log a sprint. Every sprint review, click the wall calendar and enter your team's sprint points. Points level up your character, office coder all the way to office technomancer.

🥤 Collect bobbleheads. Bits pile up each day the build stays green. Spend them at the vending machine to pull office bobbleheads, all 24 to collect.

🚨 Own your defects. Ship something broken? Pull the fire alarm. You answer one quick post-mortem question, get knocked down a level, lose a chunk of bits, and the days-since counter resets.

📋 Team to-do list. Click the board on the wall to jot down team to-dos and ideas, then check them off as you finish. The list sticks around across seasons, so revisit it at each retro.

🐉 Beat the Defect Dragon. Reach max level and collect all 24 bobbleheads to earn the keycard, then take on the Defect Dragon to win the season. Each finished season is saved under ⚙ → Seasons.
