The collection
A zippable, offline browser game. No install, no server. Just don't break the build.

How it works

📅

Log a sprint at each retro. Sprint points level you up, office coder all the way to office technomancer.

🥤

Bits pile up on their own while the build stays green. Feed them to the vending machine to pop a random office bobblehead onto your shelf, all 24 to collect.

🚨

Break something? Pull the fire alarm. The days-since counter resets, you drop a level, and lose a chunk of bits. It happens.

🐉

Hit max level with all 24 bobbleheads, then take the whole collection to the Defect Dragon to win the season.
