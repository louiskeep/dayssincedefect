How to play

📅 Log a sprint. Every sprint review, click Log a sprint and enter the team's points. Enough points auto-plants the next flower, in order, no picking. Logging also grows every plant already in the ground by one level, up to level 3.

🌳 Tend the garden. Bits pile up automatically from whatever's already planted. Click Tend the garden whenever there's enough banked to auto-plant the next tree, bigger and crazier than any flower.

🐛 Own your defects. Ship something broken? Report it. Every still-growing plant skips its next level-up, a pest event, not a currency loss.

🌸 Full Bloom. Plant and max out all 16 flowers and 8 trees to complete the season.
